## 3. A Gumroad product description prompt that stops AI product pages sounding like every other

```
Write the description for my Gumroad product page.

Product facts (use only these):
- A 4-page PDF checklist for pet sitters who visit or stay in the owner's home.
- The owner fills it in before the first visit; the sitter keeps a copy.
- Sections: feeding and medication times in one table; vet name, phone number and the most the owner will approve without calling; where things are (leash, spare key, cleaning supplies); quirks (what scares the dog, where the cat hides); a visit log to tick off.
- Works printed or filled in on a phone.
- Not for boarding kennels, which have their own intake forms.
- Price: not decided.

Rules:
- Start with one sentence saying who it is for and what changes for them.
- Then a bullet list: each bullet is the feature, then what it means for the sitter.
- Then Who it is not for and After you buy (what they download and what to do first).
- No exclamation marks. Do not use: ultimate, comprehensive, game-changing, stress-free, next level.
- Do not invent facts, numbers or promises. If something is missing, write TODO.
- Under 170 words.
```

## 7. How to check a digital product idea before building it: a prompt that returns ideas you ca

```
1. Weekly staff rota template (Google Sheets)
Who buys it: A small café or shop owner who still builds the rota on paper.
Search phrase: “staff schedule template google sheets”
Look for: Several listings for the same kind of template, from different sellers, with reviews. A price range you can read off the page.
Drop it if: Only one shop sells it, or every result is free.

2. Opening and closing checklist (printable PDF)
Who buys it: A new manager who has never been given a list.
Search phrase: “restaurant opening closing checklist”
Look for: PDF listings aimed at cafés, not only large restaurants.
Drop it if: Every result is a long generic bundle and none targets cafés. GUESS: that gap may exist.
```

## 8. How to check a digital product idea before building it: a prompt that returns ideas you ca
